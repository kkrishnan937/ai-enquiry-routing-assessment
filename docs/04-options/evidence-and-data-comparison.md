## Interpretation

### Trend-analysis limitation

Confirmed in the November 14 BA discussion: no suitable operational dataset over time is available to this project. The eight invented starter enquiries cannot establish topic frequency, changes in demand or seasonal patterns. Synthetic grouping could illustrate a method only. Record this as an evidence limitation; no additional trend demonstration or data collection is authorised by this decision. It does not establish that a real municipality lacks suitable records.

### FAQ source approach

Agreed during the November 14 BA discussion: a small set of published municipal service pages may be used as **public reference material for the portfolio exercise**. This is not internal approval of an answer collection and does not establish a municipal content owner. No FAQ source set has been selected or reviewed yet.

For each later example, retain the source URL, page title, access date and the specific information supporting the expected answer. Check applicability and visible effective dates or qualifications. Questions not supported by the selected sources should remain unanswered or be flagged for further review rather than guessed. This approach supports a limited learning test, not a claim that the FAQ option is ready for operational use.

- Available source: [starter-data documentation](../../data/README.md). It explicitly identifies provisional expectations, missing sources and absence of an evaluation split.
- Routing, FAQ assistance and guidance improvement share a dependency on reliable guidance. AI does not remove that dependency.
- No option is scored or recommended yet. Do not assume the easiest demonstration has the greatest organisational benefit.
- No actual service-performance or staff-effort improvement can be inferred from synthetic records or local prototype tests.

### Preliminary direction for discussion

Keep guidance/intake improvement as the non-AI foundation rather than assume AI replaces it. Defer real trend analysis because the necessary records are unavailable. Routing and FAQ assistance remain candidates for **one** narrow AI experiment, not two builds. Before choosing between them, identify which bounded task can obtain a defensible reference set with the least additional preparation. That is an experiment-selection decision, not proof of the greatest organisational benefit.

No winner is recorded yet. Selection should state its objective, why the alternatives were not selected for the experiment, evidence limitations and a simple baseline. Do not add another logging pilot or tracking feature to make the comparison look more complete.
