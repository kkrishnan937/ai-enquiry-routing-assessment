# Delegation of decision authority

Record: D-01  
Version: 1.0  
Issued: November 2, 2026  
Effective: November 2, 2026  
Issued by: Director, Municipal Operations  
Applies to: Roads and Waste Services in the portfolio case

## Delegation

The Roads Service Manager and Waste Services Manager may jointly approve operational guidance clarifying which of their services handles a defined enquiry type, provided the agreement stays within their existing responsibilities and resources.

If they cannot agree, the Director, Municipal Operations is the decision-maker for the disputed boundary. Submit the facts, both managers' positions, relevant existing responsibilities, and the proposed options. A decision must be recorded before it is treated as a standing rule.

This delegation does not authorise changes to statutory responsibilities, approved budgets, or another service's mandate. Matters outside this scope require separate approval through the relevant authority.

## Guidance publication

The Customer Service Supervisor may prepare and publish guidance after the relevant service managers' approval is recorded. Publication does not give the supervisor authority to decide departmental responsibilities.

The Customer Service Manager approves internal Customer Service handling instructions, including follow-up responsibility. Technical status availability and workflow effects require separate verification by the Systems Administrator.

## Limits of this record

The Waste Services team contact is not granted approval authority by this record. No decision about roadside dumping is recorded here. A joint approval or Director decision would still be needed for that particular boundary.

Record authenticity, issuing authority, scope and effective date would need verification in an actual organization. This is an authored record governed by the practice-pack disclosure.
