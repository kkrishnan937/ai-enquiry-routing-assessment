## Purpose and scope

Describe the information needed for a missed-collection referral and responsibility while information is outstanding. This article does not establish return-collection eligibility, a service-delivery commitment, or responsibility for roadside dumping.

## Proposed staff instructions

### 1. Establish the collection details

Record the collection address, cart type, and date the resident reports the collection was missed. The collection address is the property where collection was missed; it may differ from the resident's contact address. Do not substitute one for the other.

### 2. Obtain missing information

Ask the resident for missing required details before referral. Record what information was requested and what remains outstanding.

### 3. Maintain follow-up responsibility

A named Customer Service Representative retains follow-up responsibility while information is outstanding. If that representative is absent, the Customer Service Supervisor arranges named cover.

Record the responsible representative and agreed follow-up date using the permitted tracking process. Refer uncertainty about tracking or follow-up to the supervisor.

**Pending system verification:** The specific method for recording and retrieving responsibility, outstanding information, follow-up dates and cover has not been established. This draft does not prescribe a status, field, reminder or automatic reassignment.

### 4. Review the reply before referral

Check that the reply supplies the collection address, cart type and reported missed date. Having those details does not itself prove eligibility for a return collection. Apply the relevant referral procedure once that procedure is confirmed.

**Pending business review:** Confirm the referral procedure and resident-message wording. The earlier practice article says not to promise a collection date; G-01 does not separately approve that wording. No agreed sender of an acceptance message is established by this draft.

## Additional instructions proposed from the manager discussion

These points require explicit inclusion in the final review; do not describe them as decisions recorded in G-01:

- The representative and supervisor agree a follow-up date for each enquiry, considering urgency. No standard interval is established.
- The supervisor identifies cover and checks that the covering representative can access the enquiry and correspondence.
- Staff do not close an enquiry solely because the resident has not replied. Unresolved follow-up goes to the supervisor. No automatic closure period is established.
