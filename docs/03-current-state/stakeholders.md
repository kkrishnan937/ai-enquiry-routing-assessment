# Stakeholders and decision rights

Stakeholders were simulated. This table consolidates the recorded role accounts and practice decisions; it does not establish real municipal authority.

| Role | Interest in the process | What they do | Decision rights | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Resident | Have the enquiry answered or investigated | Send the enquiry and supply missing details | Not recorded in this project | [Intake steps 1–5](intake-process-summary.md#process-described) |
| Customer Service Representative | Retain enough information and a clear handoff | Read enquiries, request details, update records, refer and track until acceptance | Retains tracking responsibility; does not establish standing departmental rules | [Intake](intake-process-summary.md#responsibility-and-records), [representative account](elicitation-notes-2026-11-08.md#representative-account) |
| Customer Service Supervisor | Coordinate follow-up and absence cover | Assist with unresolved referrals, record team reasons, arrange named cover and prepare guidance | May prepare and publish guidance after relevant approvals; cannot decide departmental responsibility | [Interim direction](elicitation-notes-2026-11-08.md#interim-direction-stated-by-the-manager), [D-01](practice-records/05-delegation-record.md#guidance-publication), [G-01](practice-records/06-guidance-approval-record.md#decisions-recorded) |
| Covering representative | Continue tracking during an absence | Provide named cover arranged by the supervisor; the recording/access method remains subject to confirmation | Not recorded in this project | [G-01 proposed amendment](practice-records/06-guidance-approval-record.md#proposed-amendment) |
| Receiving service team, including Waste Services | Receive a request it can investigate | Check responsibility and information, record acceptance, then investigate | Acceptance takes responsibility for investigation; it does not establish a standing rule or collection date | [Intake steps 6–7](intake-process-summary.md#process-described), [responsibility](intake-process-summary.md#responsibility-and-records) |
| Customer Service Manager | Obtain a usable handling process and authorised guidance | Review wording and tracking readiness; coordinate unresolved cases with service managers | Approves internal Customer Service handling instructions; cannot unilaterally change another service's responsibilities | [Manager account](elicitation-notes-2026-11-08.md#manager-account-and-clarification), [D-01](practice-records/05-delegation-record.md#guidance-publication), [G-01 conditions](practice-records/06-guidance-approval-record.md#conditions-before-publication) |
| Roads Service Manager and Waste Services Manager | Set an agreed boundary within existing responsibilities | Consider departmental positions and approve standing guidance together | Joint approval within existing responsibilities and resources; Waste Services Manager approved the specified missed-collection details in G-01 | [D-01 delegation](practice-records/05-delegation-record.md#delegation), [G-01 decisions](practice-records/06-guidance-approval-record.md#decisions-recorded) |
| Director, Municipal Operations | Settle a boundary the service managers cannot agree | Review facts, positions, responsibilities and options; record a decision | Decides disputed boundaries within the delegation's scope; changes to statutory responsibilities, budgets or another mandate need separate approval | [D-01 delegation](practice-records/05-delegation-record.md#delegation) |
| Systems Administrator | Establish what the system actually supports | Verify status availability, transitions and assignment, timer or closure effects | Technical verification is separate from business approval of staff instructions | [D-01 publication](practice-records/05-delegation-record.md#guidance-publication), [G-01 decisions](practice-records/06-guidance-approval-record.md#decisions-recorded) |

## RACI

R performs the activity; A has recorded final decision authority; C supplies input; I receives the stated communication. A formal RACI was not recorded in the sources, so this is a source-based mapping. Unspecified assignments are not inferred.

| Activity | R | A | C | I | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Report missed collection | Resident | Not recorded in this project | Not recorded in this project | Customer Service Representative receives the enquiry | [Intake](intake-process-summary.md#process-described) |
| Request missing details | Customer Service Representative | Not recorded in this project | Not recorded in this project | Resident receives the request | [Intake](intake-process-summary.md#process-described) |
| Refer to team | Customer Service Representative | Not recorded in this project | Supervisor when guidance is insufficient | Resident receives the referral reference; receiving team reviews the request | [Intake](intake-process-summary.md#process-described), [elicitation](elicitation-notes-2026-11-08.md#representative-account) |
| Record acceptance | Receiving service team | Not recorded in this project | Not recorded in this project | Customer Service Representative checks the recorded acceptance | [Intake](intake-process-summary.md#responsibility-and-records) |
| Arrange absence cover | Customer Service Supervisor | Not recorded in this project | Not recorded in this project | Not recorded in this project | [G-01](practice-records/06-guidance-approval-record.md#decisions-recorded) |
| Settle disputed boundary | Customer Service Manager coordinates; service managers consider the boundary | Relevant service managers jointly, or Director, Municipal Operations if they cannot agree, within D-01 scope | Department contacts and managers provide their positions | Not recorded in this project | [Interim direction](elicitation-notes-2026-11-08.md#interim-direction-stated-by-the-manager), [D-01](practice-records/05-delegation-record.md#delegation) |

G-01 is conditional approval of proposed version 1.2, not publication authority already exercised. D-01 does not approve the roadside-dumping boundary or retrospectively approve version 1.1. The recorded system and release conditions remain separate from the completed portfolio assessment.
