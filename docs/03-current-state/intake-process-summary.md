# Current-state summary: enquiry intake and referral

Date: November 7, 2026  
Status: Working account from the Customer Service Representative agent walkthrough; checked verbally during the discussion. Procedures, screens, and request records have not been inspected. This describes the portfolio case, not verified Saskatoon operations.

## Boundary

From receipt of an email or web-form enquiry through an answer at first contact or acceptance by the responsible service team. The walkthrough explored a missed waste collection with missing information; other service areas and exceptions still need examination.

## Process described

1. The representative reads the enquiry and identifies the information needed to respond or refer it.
2. If details are missing, the representative contacts the resident. In the example, the collection address and cart type were missing.
3. The representative records what was requested, marks the enquiry as awaiting information, and retains responsibility while handling other work.
4. When the resident replies, the representative updates the record and checks the details. The example included the address, cart type, and reported missed-collection date.
5. The representative refers the request to Waste Services. Its status becomes **Referred**. The resident receives the request number and an explanation that the matter has been referred for investigation; no collection date is promised.
6. Receiving staff review whether the request belongs to their team and contains enough information. They change its status to **Accepted** when they take responsibility. The activity history records who changed it and when.
7. The representative checks outstanding referrals until acceptance. If there is no response or responsibility is disputed, the representative contacts the receiving team and then involves the supervisor as needed, recording the follow-up and updating the resident.

Information enquiries that can be answered directly were mentioned but were not walked through in detail.

## Responsibility and records

The referring representative retains responsibility during both the wait for missing details and the wait for acceptance. After acceptance, the receiving team is responsible for investigating and progressing the request. **Acceptance is not completion and does not establish a service-delivery date.**

The service-request system holds the request number, resident and enquiry details, assigned team, status, messages, and activity history. The representative uses an outstanding-referrals list rather than a separate spreadsheet. These system details are part of the walkthrough account and have not been demonstrated on screen.
